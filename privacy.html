Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

AI Chat Exporter is a free, open-source tool by Bennet Gibony. It exports your chats from Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) into a text file or a Google Doc. In short: the tool has no server and does not collect, store or share your data. Everything happens in your own browser.

1. What the tool processes

  • Your chats. When you click the bookmark on claude.ai or chatgpt.com, it reads your chats through that website, using your existing login, inside your own browser tab. They are then either saved as a file on your computer (text mode) or passed to this page in your browser to be written into your Google Drive (Google Doc mode). Your chats are never sent to the developer or to any other server.
  • Google account access (Google Doc mode only). If you choose Google Doc mode, you sign in with Google and grant the drive.file permission. It lets this page create new Google Docs in your Drive and edit only the files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive. The sign-in token stays in your browser tab and is discarded when you close it.

2. Use of Google user data

Data received from Google APIs is used only to create the document you asked for in your own Google Drive. It is not stored, transferred to anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. AI Chat Exporter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. What the developer receives

Nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising on this site.

4. Third parties

  • Hosting: the site is served by Cloudflare Pages and/or GitHub Pages. Like any web host, they process your IP address and technical request data to deliver the page. See their privacy policies.
  • Google: only if you choose Google Doc mode, the page loads Google's sign-in library and talks to the Google Docs API. Google's privacy policy applies to that.
  • Anthropic / OpenAI: the bookmark talks to claude.ai or chatgpt.com, which you are already logged into, the same way the website itself does.

5. Verify it yourself

The complete source code is public at github.com/bennetgibony/AI_account_migrator, and the published page is automatically checked to match it.

6. Your rights and contact

Because no personal data is collected or stored by the developer, there is no data to access, correct or delete. For questions, please open an issue on GitHub.
